Methodology

Logistics Assessment

1 What is a Logistics Assessment?

  • It is a structured, in-depth evaluation of supply chain planning and warehouse processes.
  • Rather than relying solely on high-level administrative reports, an audit analyzes physical movements, information flows, and actual shop-floor execution.
  • Its primary goal is to map performance against KPIs to quantify the economic impact of operational bottlenecks.

2 Identifying Hidden Waste and Costs

  • Warehouse & Handling Outlines how poor layouts, disorganized picking routes, or inadequate stock management increase labor costs and extend cycle times.
  • Transport & Delivery Examines extra charges, failed delivery attempts, transit bottlenecks, and customer complaints that inflate overall logistics spend.
  • Information Systems (ERP/WMS) Highlights the gap between software data and reality—data may be correct on paper, yet fail to highlight underlying operational delays.

3 Benefits

  • Cost Reduction: Eliminates non-value-added activities and hidden operational leaks.
  • Improved Service Quality: Reduces delays, errors, and delivery failures, boosting overall customer satisfaction.
  • Data-Driven Decision Making: Translates operational flaws directly into financial metrics to help management prioritize corrective actions.
